Gen-Z Starting Out: Holly, Janice and the £10-a-Week Beginning

What the youngest UK money creators teach about starting from zero

← All money advice Getting started

Starting young beats starting big. The youngest UK money creators — plus the veterans quoting small beginnings — make the case for starting now with whatever you have.

Holly: 20 and learning in public

Holly Ann (@holl_005, 6.5K followers) is "20 & travelling Asia… learning money, markets & life." Her content proves you can build financial literacy at any age — and that documenting the journey keeps you accountable.

Janice: all things money and investing

Janice (@janiice.myla, 1.7K followers) talks "all things money and investing" — small account, serious topics. Early creators often give the most relatable beginner advice because they're living it.

£10 a week still counts

Sean (@seans.money): "If you're waiting until you have more money to invest, you're doing it wrong. I'm doing it with just £10 a week."

— Sean (TikTok source ↗)

Build habits before you have money

Sean again: "It's important to build the habits of investing early… so that when you do have more money, you understand how the stock market works."

— Sean (TikTok source ↗)

£100 with your sister

Mia Rose (@miarosemcgrath): "Me and my sister start investing with £100! Who ends up with more?" Making it social and competitive turns a chore into a game.

— Mia Rose (TikTok source ↗)

Track every pound first

Ollie Invests (@ollieinvests): only 54% of UK adults track where their money goes — he uses Monzo's budgeting tools to know exactly where every pound lands before investing a penny of it.

— Ollie Invests (TikTok source ↗)

The one rule

Open a Stocks & Shares ISA with £10, buy a global index fund, automate it monthly. Increase when you can. Time does the rest.
