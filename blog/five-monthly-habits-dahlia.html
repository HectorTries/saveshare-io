5 Monthly Money Habits That Keep Dahlia Five Steps Ahead

Know your numbers, automate your bills and investments, plus Tatiana's budget-with-me transparency and Janice's £10K uni-portfolio system

Dahlia (Dahlia) opens with the problem every payday brings: Her answer is five habits, all systems, no willpower. "I direct debit 10% of my monthly take home pay into my investing account automatically every month. That way I keep growing my investment pot without having to think about transferring that money across. At the moment, my primary investment allocation is in VWRP, which is the Vanguard FTSE All World, my money is automatically invested into over 3,800 global companies across every sector. But most importantly, I never have to think about where I'm investing my money and I can just get on with my life." Contract-renewal alerts kill the loyalty penalty: UK regulators estimate loyal customers overpay hundreds of pounds a year on energy, broadband and insurance. Pair that with an emergency buffer and the system is complete: fixed costs known, bills automated, investing automated, renewals challenged.

Tatiana (Tatiana) proves habit 1 in public every month: She then walks through every fixed expense, £650 rent (living at home), £63 life insurance, £46 critical illness, £28 phone, £22 gym, £37 subscriptions. Radical line-by-line transparency is what makes Dahlia's habit 1 real rather than aspirational.

Janice (Janice) is the student-budget proof of habit 3: Same system as Dahlia, smaller numbers, same compounding engine.

Know every outgoing, pay every bill on the 1st, and auto-invest a fixed slice into one global fund, then get on with your life.
