Women and Money: Amy, Kafilat, DiDi and the Creators Closing the Gap

Systems, investing and confidence — advice from the women of UK money TikTok

← All money advice Women & money

The UK investing gap is real — women hold less in investments and pensions than men. A wave of UK creators is changing that, one video at a time.

For women that want more

Amy — Financially Her (@financiallyher_, 45.6K followers) creates content "for women that want more — from their career, money & life." Her angle: money confidence starts with treating your finances as a project worth ambition.

Systems for millennial women earning well

Kafilat (@kafilat.finance, 7.3K followers) helps "millennial women earning well build systems" for their money — because high earners without systems still end months at zero. Automation first, guilt never.

Become financially free together

DiDi (@didifinance, 18.2K followers): "Let's become financially free together." Community-driven content that makes investing feel social rather than solitary.

Buy the market and move on

Sophie Blank (@itssophieblank), £230K net worth: "Rich girls don't panic and pick stocks, they buy the market and move on. Every month, I buy exactly 3 funds."

— Sophie Blank (TikTok source ↗)

Curate it — Hannah's systems

Hannah Bevington (@hannahbevington), £135K at 26: "Building wealth doesn't just happen. You really have to curate it and put certain systems in place."

— Hannah Bevington (TikTok source ↗)

Sinking funds — Sophie's debt shield

"My sinking funds are the one reason I pay off my credit card every single month and I never go into debt." Pots for known future spending are the unglamorous engine of financial control.

— Sophie Blank (TikTok source ↗)

The one rule

Open the ISA, automate the monthly purchase, and talk about money openly. Visibility compounds too.
