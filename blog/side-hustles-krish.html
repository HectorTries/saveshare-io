Side Hustles That Actually Build Wealth: Krish Kara's Playbook and Friends

From content creation to second incomes — what creators do beyond the 9-to-5

← All money advice Side hustles

Krish Kara (@krish.kara, 104K followers) built his audience on money tips, investing and side hustles — the trio that turns spare hours into invested capital.

Never rely on one income

Mia Rose (@miarosemcgrath): "Having a side hustle really helped me. I don't ever want to be reliant on one source of income, especially in this economy." Her side hustle was content creation alongside a fashion 9-to-5.

— Mia Rose (TikTok source ↗)

Start investing with £100 — then scale

Sophie Blank (@itssophieblank): "I made my first investment of £100 in the summer of 2018." Eight years later: £230,000 net worth. Small starts compound; side-hustle income accelerates them.

— Sophie Blank (TikTok source ↗)

£10 a week counts

Sean (@seans.money): "If you're waiting until you have more money to invest, you're doing it wrong. I'm doing it with just £10 a week." Side-hustle cash maps perfectly onto this: earn a little extra, invest all of it.

— Sean (TikTok source ↗)

Invest variably, like Hannah

Hannah Bevington (@hannahbevington): "I invest whatever I can. Sometimes it's £50 a month, other times it's two, three, four thousand." Irregular hustle income? Invest it irregularly — just keep investing.

— Hannah Bevington (TikTok source ↗)

The one rule

Direct 100% of side-hustle income to investments until your emergency fund and debts are sorted — then keep doing it anyway.
