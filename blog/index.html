Saving

Pay yourself first with systems, not willpower

“Building wealth doesn't just happen. You really have to curate it and put certain systems in place.”Hannah Bevington (TikTok ↗)
“My sinking funds are the one reason I pay off my credit card every single month and I never go into debt.”Sophie Blank (TikTok ↗)

Kill high-interest debt before anything else

“Get rid of high interest debt. I'm talking credit cards, Klarna.”Mia Rose (TikTok ↗)
“Once I paid [my student loan] off, my net worth skyrocketed.”Sophie Blank (TikTok ↗)

“Put away [money] in your emergency fund in a high yield savings account.”Ollie Invests (TikTok ↗)

Investing

Buy the market, don't pick stocks

“Rich girls don't panic and pick stocks, they buy the market and move on. Every month, I buy exactly 3 funds.”Sophie Blank (TikTok ↗)
“I just wanna match the all world. If I do that, I actually will beat most [stock pickers].”Neil Invests (TikTok ↗)
“Use ETFs, diversify your investments and think long term… 70% of my money goes straight into a FTSE world fund.”Ollie Invests (TikTok ↗)

Contributions matter more than returns (at first)

“The biggest thing that's gonna impact your portfolio performance for a number of years is your contributions into it, not the returns.”Neil Invests (TikTok ↗)
“It's important to build the habits of investing early… so that when you do have more money, you understand how the stock market works.”Sean (TikTok ↗)
“I invest whatever I can. Sometimes it's £50 a month, other times it's two, three, four thousand.”Hannah Bevington (TikTok ↗)

“Saving money was keeping me broke… inflation [is] constantly eating away at the purchasing power of your money.”Iain Geddes (TikTok ↗)
“Inflation erodes the buying power of our money. £10,000 today is not worth the same as £10,000 will be in the future.”Mia Rose (TikTok ↗)
“I was putting the effort in, but I was getting nowhere fast… saving and investing are two completely different things.”Iain Geddes (TikTok ↗)

Don't panic-sell, think in decades

“Do not panic sell… Remember why you invested in them in the first place. Think of that long-term picture.”Iain Geddes (TikTok ↗)
“Keep that longterm mindset!”Iain Geddes (TikTok ↗)
