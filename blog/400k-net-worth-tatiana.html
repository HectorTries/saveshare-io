For a higher-rate taxpayer, a £1,000 pension contribution via salary sacrifice can cost as little as £580 in take-home pay once 40% income tax, 2% National Insurance and the employer's NI saving (often passed on) are factored in. That is an instant 70%+ uplift before markets move at all, no investment can reliably match it. Tatiana's decision to double down on her pension this year is textbook: shelter the maximum from the taxman, collect the employer match, and let global equities compound inside the wrapper. The annual allowance (£60,000 for most people) caps the party, but few under 35 come close, meaning every spare pound up to the employer-match limit is essentially free money refused if left on the table.

Scale the principles down and they still work.
