FAQ

Where do the net worth figures come from?

They're self-described — stated by each creator in their own TikTok videos. We extract them from transcripts and link back to the source so you can verify.

Are the figures verified?

No independent verification. Figures may be outdated or aspirational. Treat them as what the creator claims, not audited fact.

How often is data refreshed?

Stats and transcripts are re-polled every Monday. Last pull date is shown in the footer.

Is this financial advice?

No. Nothing on this site constitutes financial advice. Do your own research.
