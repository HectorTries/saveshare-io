Methodology

Source. Each figure was stated by the creator it belongs to, in their own content, and links to that source so you can check it yourself.

Stated only. Net worth means the creator said the words about themselves. Hypothetical projections, worked examples, other people’s money and generic illustrations are excluded. Where a creator gave a range or a round description such as five figures, the floor of that statement is shown and the quote says so.

Labels. Property purchase prices are labelled property value, not net worth. Monthly and yearly flows are labelled income flow, not totals. A remaining balance owed is labelled debt balance, and an amount cleared is labelled debt payoff.

No fabrication. Where no figure was stated, nothing is estimated and no chart is drawn. Averages and medians use only creators with a stated figure of that type.

Currency. UK figures in pounds, US figures in dollars. No conversion is applied anywhere on this page.

Freshness. Figures carry the date they were stated. Markets move and creators update their numbers, so treat every figure as a dated snapshot.
