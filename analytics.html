Methodology

Source. Figures come from archived TikTok transcripts (753 UK videos, 387 US videos) plus on-screen portfolio screenshots verified by OCR, and creator-written captions. Research notes: networth-research.md, networth-verification-2026-10-05.md, networth-deep-2026-10-05.md, networth-ocr-2026-10-05.md.

Self-described only. Net worth means the creator said the words about themselves. Hypothetical projections, other people's money, and generic examples are excluded. Property purchase prices are labelled property value, not net worth. Monthly flows are labelled as flows, not totals.

No fabrication. Where no figure was stated, the value is not disclosed. Averages and medians use only creators with a stated net worth figure. Combined invested totals sum stated portfolio and invested amounts only, in original currency.

Currency. UK figures in pounds, US figures in dollars. No conversion applied in the tables. Reference rate used only for narrative comparison: about 1.27 USD per GBP (Oct 2026).

Freshness. Figures carry the date stated. Markets move and creators update their numbers, so treat every figure as a snapshot. Last rebuilt 12 Oct 2026.
