Who is Tiffany Morrison?

Tiffany Morrison is the US money creator behind @singlemombudgeting, sharing single-mom budgeting on one income. At 40, after nearly 20 years of full-time work, she was laid off and decided to build multiple streams of income through content creation, brand partnerships, and handmade crochet products — including crocheted hanging plants — to keep a roof over her head and food on the table for her and her 17-year-old daughter.

How they got there

Tiffany says she worked full-time for nearly 20 years before suddenly losing her paycheck. With Florida unemployment at only $275 a week — not enough to cover rent — she set a goal of bringing in around $3,500 a month to stay afloat. She is documenting the journey of replacing her $60,000-a-year salary with a handmade business, leaning on years of content creation and brand work plus crochet skills, and sharing the ups and downs of budgeting through a hard season of life.
