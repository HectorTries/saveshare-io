Who is Cazza Smith?

Cameron 'Cazza' Smith is a verified UK personal finance creator behind @cazzatime (436K followers, 9.4M likes, 755 videos), whose bio reads "School didn't teach you about money - Here's what you should've been taught". A University of York graduate, he started investing at 21 when he began creating personal finance content. His videos cover saving systems, ISAs, mortgages and Budget explainers.

How they got there

His November 2019 bank statement, shared when he was 20 and at university, showed £6,000 sitting in his current account, which he says was his entire net worth at the time, with nothing going into savings or investments. He started investing at 21 and went on to save £27,740 in under three years using a pay-yourself-first system. After renting privately for three years he bought his home about two years ago. He says around £280,000 remains on the mortgage with almost £100,000 of equity built, bringing the loan to value to about 75%. In October 2026 he remortgaged onto a two-year fix at 4.8%, cutting repayments to £1,508 a month and saving about £60 a month.
