Who is Alisa Glutz?

Alisa Glutz is a US money creator (@colormycredit) focused on credit education. A licensed loan officer (Arizona, California, Georgia, Texas, Florida and Illinois), she explains credit scores, mortgage scoring models and money habits in plain language, including a FinCon 2026 talk using fantasy football to teach credit-score habits (Oct 2026).

How they got there

Alisa works as a licensed loan officer and speaks openly about pushing through fear to teach money topics, including her 2026 FinCon presentation “Turning Boring Money Topics Into Viral Content.” Her signature approach uses fantasy football to explain credit habits: pay every bill on time, keep card balances low, pay balances down before the statement closes, be careful closing older accounts, check reports for mistakes, and build a score through consistent decisions over time. She also covers mortgage news such as lenders now being able to use VantageScore 4.0 alongside Classic FICO for eligible conventional loans, and urges borrowers to ask their loan officer which score model is being used.
