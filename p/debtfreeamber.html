Who is Amber?

Amber is the US money creator behind @debtfreeamber, documenting her journey out of $85,413.65 of debt. Aged 33, single with no kids, she is a NYC transplant with two roommates working a full-time cosmetics job, sharing paycheck breakdowns, grocery hauls on tight weekly budgets, and honest budgeting updates.

How they got there

Amber says she went almost three years making little to no money before starting work as a cosmetics manager at Macy's in New York in late March, roughly doubling what she earned back home in Columbus, Ohio. Her April net pay totalled $3,582 across four weekly paychecks. She is now working through her credit cards — currently on her second — while stretching every dollar with budgets like $60 a week for groceries.
