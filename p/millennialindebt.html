Who is Melissa Jean-Baptiste?

Melissa Jean-Baptiste is a US money creator (@millennialindebt) who paid off $100,000 in student loans on a teacher salary. A former New York City high school teacher turned investing educator and author, she runs Millennial In Debt (9 years old as of Oct 2026) and a free Future Millionaire newsletter teaching beginners how to start investing, and calls herself the Beyonce of personal finance (self-described, Oct 2026).

How they got there

Melissa paid off $100,000 in student loans while working as a teacher, then went full throttle into entrepreneurship before deciding that full-time entrepreneurship was not for her and returning to the stability of teaching alongside her business. At 38, she says she is on track to retire with $3.3 million, and she teaches investing step-by-step for beginners — from opening a 401(k) and an IRA to building a three-fund portfolio and avoiding fund overlap.
