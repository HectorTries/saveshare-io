Who is Kathryn?

Budgeting, personal finance, money mindset. 2026 bride.

Kathryn is a UK budgeting creator who shares her full payday routine on camera: exact pay, exact bills, exact savings pots. Freshly married, she is paying off about £3,600 of wedding credit card debt by reselling clothes on Vinted, and she reports back on her progress video by video.

How they got there

Her September payday video lays out the whole picture: £2,570.49 monthly pay, £1,593.62 in her half of the bills plus personal bills, a £600 lump sum toward the wedding card, £100 a month into a Fidelity stocks and shares ISA (rising to £150), £800 split across six savings pots, and around £250 of disposable income left over.

She plans a wedding for under £4.5K, overpays her electric bill to build a winter cushion (about £170 in credit), and runs six named savings pots alongside the debt payoff. Her niche is honest beginner budgeting with every pound accounted for.

Payday routine style

Kathryn's standout trait is showing the full maths: pay in, bills out, debt payment, ISA contribution, six pots, disposable income left. Nothing is rounded away. For viewers starting their own budget, her videos double as a template: list every fixed cost, fund the pots first, and let the leftover be the spending money rather than the other way round.
