Who is Structured Life Co?

UK debt payoff creator running a daily series: secretly paying off 39K of debt without her husband noticing.

Each day she makes a payment based on the previous video's engagement (25p per like, 50p per follow), tops it up with Vinted and Marketplace sales plus viewer donations, then renames the bank statement transaction to something silly so her husband does not spot it. The series runs from day 21 to day 39, with side hustle income trackers sold on Etsy to fund extra payments.

How they got there

Her format is simple and public: a fixed daily payment rule tied to engagement, petty disguises on the bank statement (from Specsavers to Woolworths to Blockbusters), and honest recaps of the running total.

By day 25 she had paid off 582.46 in 25 days, and after updating all her balances she reported over 1,000 paid off in September alone. The running target is 39,000 of debt, paid down one disguised payment at a time.
