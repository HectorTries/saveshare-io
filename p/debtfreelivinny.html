Who is Cheryl Vallejo?

Cheryl Vallejo is a US money creator (@debtfreelivinny) focused on debt free living in new york. Cheryl Vallejo, debt free living educator (Oct 2026).

How they got there

Cheryl teaches practical, realistic budgeting: giving every dollar a job with a zero-balance budget, paying off debt with the debt snowball, and stretching the grocery budget with meal prep. She sells budget planners and binders (her "Queen of All Planners" meal prep sheet) via her Stan store and rallies her followers as fellow "budgeting queens" — encouraging them to try again, keep going, and recover financially with intention.
