Who is Jake Shuttleworth?

Jake Shuttleworth is a 31 year old UK creator documenting his life and self-improvement journey on TikTok. His niche is honest debt-free content: how he fell into about £23,000 of debt, the graft it took to clear it in roughly 18 months, and how he plans to stay debt free while building savings.

His most watched video, seen more than 32,000 times, is part two of his debt story, covering everything he learned since clearing the debt and how he plans to stay out of it.

How they got there

Jake paid off about £23,000 of debt in roughly 18 months through graft, planning, and a year-long budgeting spreadsheet covering every outgoing. His three-step method: write down every debt, write down every monthly expense, then budget for a full 12 months ahead rather than one month at a time.

Now debt free, he is on a road to 3K savings challenge, aiming to save three grand by the end of the year, while documenting the self-improvement habits, like quitting drinking, that keep him on track.
