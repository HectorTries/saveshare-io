Apprentice route edge

Grace shows the debt free route into law that most careers advice skips. Her most useful videos compare the two routes directly and name the tradeoff honestly: the solicitor path pays off, but quitting early leaves you with nothing to show for it. For school leavers who cannot face £50K of university debt, her path is the worked example.
