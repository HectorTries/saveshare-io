Who is Georgia May Thornley?

Georgia May Thornley is a UK money creator who posts as Georgia May | Blonde Ambition, documenting the unglamorous middle of a money journey rather than the finished trophy version. She is 27, started her first job at 14 on £3.79 an hour, and has worked ever since. With around 6,300 followers, her content is closer to a diary than a masterclass: credit card debt, an emergency fund being rebuilt, a £26,000 salary she has publicly defended, and the recurring temptation to spend rather than save. She describes her past self bluntly, including a period of what she calls a severe spending addiction, and now makes videos about the habits that replaced it.
