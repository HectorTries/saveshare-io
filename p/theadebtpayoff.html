Who is Thea?

Thea is a UK creator on a mission to be debt free with her husband by 30, so they can save for a house and start trying for a baby. She posts weekly roundups, side hustle experiments (surveys, UGC, AttaPoll, Noise) and honest wobbles, including a break she took before coming back to the payoff plan.

How she is doing it

Thea works a part time job, picks up extra shifts for bonuses, and funnels spare cash plus side hustle income into extra debt payments. She worked out she needs an extra 168 GBP a month on top of minimums to clear one loan by December, and paid one loan down to 906 GBP with seven months left. Her content is weekly accountability: wins, slip ups, holidays she refuses to feel guilty about, and the maths of small extra payments.
