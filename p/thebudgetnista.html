Who is Tiffany Aliche?

Tiffany Aliche is the US money creator known as The Budgetnista. Her message centers on living under your means rather than looking wealthy: she would rather have a real gap between what she earns and spends, stocking away excess income so that money swings do not become lifestyle swings.

How they got there

Tiffany says that during the pandemic her company was making ten million dollars in a year, and post-pandemic it makes about half that. She never lived up to the ten-million level — instead she stocked the excess away. That cushion is why, she says, she now has enough wealth that as long as she is not trying to do anything extravagant, she does not have to work anymore.
