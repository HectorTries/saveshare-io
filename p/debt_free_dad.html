Who is Brad Nelson?

Brad Nelson is a US money creator (@debt_free_dad) focused on debt free dad, budgeting systems. Debt Free Dad, budgeting and debt payoff systems (Oct 2026).

How they got there

Brad teaches a habit-first approach to getting out of debt: setting up savings buckets (sinking funds) for predictable future expenses like car repairs, holidays and school supplies, so surprise bills stop landing on the credit card. His message is that spending awareness, catching overspending early, and learning to say no to yourself are signs of real progress, and that consistency beats perfection on the debt-free journey.
